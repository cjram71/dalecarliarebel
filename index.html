help Questions Parents Ask

What ages are welcome? Can girls play? expand_more

American football is for everyone! We welcome boys and girls across all youth and junior age groups. Contact us to find the right practice group for your child.

Is previous experience required? expand_more

None whatsoever. Most of our players started with zero football background. Our certified coaches teach every stance, step, and drill from square one.

How is contact introduced safely? expand_more

Beginners learn body control, falling safely, and spatial awareness before any contact exercises. Safety according to SWE3 national guidelines with trained coaches is always our #1 priority.

What equipment is needed for the first session? expand_more

Just athletic workout clothes, a water bottle, and soccer cleats or running shoes. The club provides helmets and shoulder pads for loan during introductory sessions.
