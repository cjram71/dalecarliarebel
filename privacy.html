Privacy & GDPR

Privacy Policy

Dalecarlia Rebels AFF values your privacy. Our website is designed to provide public club information with minimal data processing and zero third-party tracking.

What this site does not use

  • No advertising or tracking cookies
  • No analytics tools that profile individual users
  • No user accounts or local cookie storage
  • No hidden trackers or third-party pixel beacons

Contact & Registration Forms

When you fill out the interest registration form on the homepage, your data is processed locally in your web browser. The tool prepares an email for your device's native email client (mailto:) or lets you copy the summary to your clipboard. You maintain full control over what is sent.

External Links

Links to social media platforms (Facebook, Instagram) and partner federations (SWE3, Falu Energi & Vatten) lead to external websites with their own independent privacy practices.
