# Dalecarlia Rebels English Website

Official English-language website for `https://dalecarliarebels.com/`.

## Purpose

- Recruit youth players, adult beginners, and returning athletes in Falun and Borlänge, Sweden.
- Provide useful parent, equipment, and safety information.
- Showcase the 2026 Season roadmap, Summer Camp, and Dukes Tourney participation.
- Strengthen the Dalecarlia Rebels club identity and sponsor proposition.
- Route English-speaking and international visitors to the club contact email: `dalecarliarebelsaff@gmail.com`.
- Connect reciprocally to the Swedish website at `https://dalecarliarebels.se/`.

## Architecture

- Modern, athletic responsive platform built with Tailwind CSS, Barlow Condensed, and Outfit typography.
- Interactive Position Finder with 6 archetypes and dynamic position recommendations.
- Youth & Parents pathway featuring age-adapted safety pillars and FAQ accordion.
- Fast local pre-registration intake form generating direct emails to `dalecarliarebelsaff@gmail.com`.
- Fully privacy-preserving without third-party tracking or advertising cookies.
